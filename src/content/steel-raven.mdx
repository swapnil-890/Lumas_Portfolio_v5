## Problem

Commercial quadcopter drones are optimized for stability, not agility. Biological corvids (crows, ravens) demonstrate superior flight characteristics:

- Rapid direction changes
- Efficient gliding
- Perching on irregular surfaces
- Load carrying relative to body weight

**Goal:** Design a drone architecture that mimics corvid flight mechanics.

## Approach

### Biomimetic Principles

| Biological Feature | Engineering Translation |
|--------------------|------------------------|
| Variable wing sweep | Articulated arm geometry |
| Tail feathers | Stabilator surface |
| Perching claws | Passive grip mechanism |
| Lightweight skeleton | Carbon fiber truss frame |

### Material Selection

**Frame:** Carbon fiber reinforced polymer (CFRP)

| Material | Specific Strength | Cost | Machinability |
|----------|-------------------|------|---------------|
| Aluminum 6061 | 77 kN·m/kg | Low | Easy |
| Titanium Ti-6Al-4V | 213 kN·m/kg | High | Hard |
| CFRP | 2187 kN·m/kg | Medium | Medium |

**Chosen:** CFRP for primary structure, aluminum for motor mounts.

### Frame Geometry

**Wheelbase:** 220mm (diagonal motor-to-motor)

**Arm angle:** 45° forward sweep (mimics corvid wing position)

**Arm cross-section:** I-beam profile (optimized stiffness-to-weight)

## The Hardest 5%: Weight vs. Payload Trade-off

**Constraint:** Every gram added to frame reduces payload capacity.

**Analysis:**

```
Total thrust budget: ~2400g (4x 600g motors)
Target hover weight: <450g (AUW)
Available for payload: ~1950g theoretical
Practical payload (50% control authority): ~300g

Compute module budget: 150g
- Pi Zero 2 W: 10g
- Camera: 5g
- IMU: 2g
- Wiring: 15g
- Mounting: 20g
- Battery (separate): 50g
- Margin: 48g
```

**Design approach:** Frame weight reduced through:
1. I-beam arm profile (estimated 28% weight reduction vs solid)
2. Topology optimization of frame plate
3. CFRP motor mounts where possible

## Design Challenges (Open)

### Challenge 1: Arm-Root Stress Concentration

**Concern:** Sharp corners at arm-root junction create stress concentration points.

**Planned mitigation:** Fillet radius (3mm) at junction. Requires FEA validation before fabrication.

### Challenge 2: Motor Vibration Isolation

**Concern:** Frame resonance may overlap with motor RPM frequencies.

**Planned mitigation:** TPU dampeners between motor mounts and arms. Resonance characterization needed after first prototype.

### Challenge 3: GPS Antenna Placement

**Concern:** Carbon fiber frame may block GPS signals. 3D printed canopy material selection affects RF performance.

**Planned mitigation:** PETG canopy with antenna cutout. Needs testing.
